TrailBuddy — Delete your account

How to delete your account and all data that belongs to it. This is a courtesy translation; the German version is binding.

In the app

  1. Open TrailBuddy and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll all the way down to Delete account.
  4. Type your own username to confirm and choose Delete for good.

The same path works in the web app — there without installing anything.

What is deleted

Immediately and irreversibly, without a grace period:

  • your profile including username and email address,
  • all your trail recordings and contributions (names, ratings, condition reports),
  • your buddy connections and open requests,
  • your messages to buddies and theirs to you — the conversation is gone for both sides.

A trail only stays for others if they have recorded it themselves — your recording of it is gone afterwards. Trails that only you had recorded disappear completely.

One exception: feedback you sent from the app may end up publicly in the GitHub project and cannot be taken back by deleting the account. In that case, please contact the operator directly.

No longer able to sign in?

If you can no longer sign in, write to pilzbuddy@proton.me — the account is then deleted by hand. Please not through the public GitHub issues: there your request would be readable by anyone.

How your data is handled is set out in the privacy policy; who is behind TrailBuddy, in the legal notice.
